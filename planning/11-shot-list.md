## 3 · How to speak it

**Pace: 130 wpm.** Slow deliberately in three places — the enforcement list at
1:05, the verdict at ~2:24, and the last sentence.

**Pronunciation:** *NOAA's HYSPLIT* → **"NO-ah's HISS-split."** Say it twice now.

**Breathe here:**
- after "…fines on individual farmers," — it's a four-item list, beat between each
- after "calendar" in *"two things following the calendar track each other…"*
- after "says" in *"our reconstruction says the arriving air hadn't come…"*
- before "One thousand three hundred and eighty-four" — six number-words, take a run-up

**Stress these or they mean nothing:**
- *"a **change** in burning predicts a **change** in pollution"* — both words
- *"we wrote down what would count as success **before** we ran it"*
- *"That last one **is** the result"*

### ⚠️ The dangerous moment — ~2:24

> *"And by that standard, all four specifications failed."*

A judge who stops here hears defeat. Four things with your voice:

1. **Stress "standard", not "failed."** The sentence is about the test, not the outcome.
2. **Do not drop pitch at the end.** Falling intonation reads as an apology. Keep it level, like reading a measurement off an instrument.
3. **Do not slow down.** Slowing signals bad news. Same tempo as the sentence before.
4. **Small pause, then straight into** *"Our index never reached significance in any of them"* — same flat register. **Then** a real pause, and lift slightly for *"That's not a failed project."*

Think: a scientist reading out a result they expected might happen. Not a student admitting a mistake.

**The last 5 seconds have no narration. Do not stop the recording when you stop talking.**

## 4 · If it goes wrong

**Over 5:00 on a timed rehearsal — drop these two, in order:**
1. *"The faint lines are seven alternative starting points — that's our uncertainty."* (12 words, ~6s). The on-screen legend already says it.
2. *"That's called pre-registering: it means you can't quietly go looking for the version that works."* (16 words, ~7s). Only if you've already said "wrote down what would count as success."

**Never cut:** "Why this matters", the four-cell failure, the farmers beat, or *"That last one is the result."*

**Fumble mid-take:** push through if it's one word. Restart only if you lost a whole sentence. **Natural restart points** are the section starts at 0:22, 1:48, 2:36 and 4:01 — each begins a new visual, so a cut there is invisible.

**Animation stutters:** stop, hard-reload, wait 15s before restarting. It is almost always a cold shader compile on first load.

**A beat mistimes** (you finish narration before the visual lands): hold silent rather than filling. Silence over a moving map reads as confident; filler does not.

## 5 · After the take

**Runtime:** check the file duration. **Under 5:00 or it does not go in.** 4:40–4:55 is the target.

**On playback, check in this order:**
1. Runtime under 5:00
2. Audio audible throughout, no clipping on the loud consonants
3. The path is visible and legible when the video is compressed
4. The ledger district names are readable at playback size
5. The ~2:24 verdict does not sound like an apology
6. The final frame is the map, held, silent

**Minimum acceptable to submit:** under 5:00, audible, animation plays through
once cleanly, the four-cell table is on screen while you say all four failed,
and the last frame is the map. Everything else is polish.

**Record twice regardless.** Keep the second. The first take is where you find
out which sentences you cannot say.
